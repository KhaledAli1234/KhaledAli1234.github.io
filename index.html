Auto Care

A full-stack car management platform. The phone records a trip, three machine learning services score the driving, predict vehicle health and estimate fuel use, and a chatbot answers questions from the driver's own data.

  • I built the NestJS backend: JWT authentication with role-based access, and the vehicle, maintenance, fuel-log and GPS trip modules.
  • I also built support tickets and Socket.IO notifications between drivers and admins.
  • I integrated the Flask and Groq assistant (Llama 3.3 70B) as a microservice and containerized every service with Docker.
  • A unified service fans each trip out to three models: Random Forest for driving style, Linear Regression for vehicle health and Gradient Boosting for fuel use.
  • The client is a React Native app built with Expo that also runs on the web.
